Service Terms & Privacy Policy

SW Tyre Man · South West England & South Wales · Last Updated: 24 August 2026

1. Emergency Roadside Dispatch

• Arrival Times: Quoted arrival estimates (e.g. 30–60 minutes) are targets provided in good faith and depend on live traffic conditions, weather, and incidents across the M4, M5, Severn crossings, and surrounding road networks.

• Roadside Safety: If you are stranded on a motorway hard shoulder or active carriageway, you must remain behind safety barriers where available. Technicians reserve the right to delay or abort fitting if on-site safety cannot be maintained.

• Cancellations: Once a dedicated technician van has been dispatched to your location, standard call-out charges apply if the job is cancelled en route.

• Customer-Supplied Information: It is the customer's responsibility to provide the correct tyre size and vehicle location. If a technician arrives on-site and the tyre requested does not fit the vehicle due to customer error, standard call-out charges apply.

2. Pricing & Payment Terms

• Payment is due strictly on-site upon completion of tyre fitting via card machine or pre-agreed digital invoice.

• Quoted emergency pricing covers the tyre, mobile fitting, standard rubber valve replacement, balancing, and environmental tyre disposal unless stated otherwise.

• Vehicles equipped with specialized electronic TPMS valves or locking nut removal services may incur standard additional component fees.

3. Wheel Safety, Repairs & Liabilities

• Locking Wheel Nut Keys: Customers must supply the correct locking wheel nut key on arrival. We accept no liability for damage resulting from previously overtightened, corroded, or damaged wheel bolts/keys installed by third parties.

• Puncture Repairs (BS AU 159): Puncture repairs are carried out strictly according to British Standard BS AU 159. If a tyre is deemed unrepairable due to shoulder/sidewall penetration, run-flat driving, or internal carcass separation, a replacement tyre must be fitted for road safety.

• Pre-Existing Rim & TPMS Conditions: We are not liable for pre-existing cracked alloys, structural rim buckles, bead corrosion preventing an airtight seal, or seized/faulty TPMS valves.

• Wheel Retorque Notice: As standard UK automotive safety best practice, customers are advised to inspect and re-torque wheel nuts after driving approximately 30–50 miles following mobile tyre replacement.

• Warranty: All new tyres supplied carry standard manufacturer warranties covering material or manufacturing defects. Warranties strictly exclude road hazard damage (potholes, curbing, punctures, or uneven tread wear from suspension misalignment).

4. Location Data & UK GDPR Privacy

Strict Permission-Only Location Access

We never access, track, or collect your GPS location automatically without your explicit consent. Your browser or mobile device will strictly prompt you for permission ("Allow SW Tyre Man to access your location?") when you submit your dispatch request.

• Explicit Consent Only: Location coordinates are only retrieved if you actively click "Allow" on your device prompt. If you deny or dismiss the prompt, zero location data is accessed, and we rely purely on the address or junction you typed.

• Single-Use Dispatch Purpose: Coordinates are used solely to generate your emergency dispatch message (via WhatsApp or SMS) so the technician can navigate directly to your vehicle.

• UK GDPR Compliance: Customer contact details and invoice records are processed solely for fulfilling roadside services and statutory accounting/tax records in compliance with the Data Protection Act 2018. We never sell, lease, or distribute customer details to third-party marketing networks.
